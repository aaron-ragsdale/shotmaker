Shoot with your arm

ShotLab reads your shooting arm through the camera. Stand 4 to 8 feet back so your shoulder, elbow, and wrist are in view.

  1. Bend your elbow a little to load the shot.
  2. Lift your wrist and extend. A short upward motion is enough.
  3. Behind view: point your arm left or right of the hoop to aim.

About

How it works. Bend your elbow to load, then extend and raise your wrist. The game measures a short release window. Wrist motion and elbow loading affect distance; forearm tilt affects arc; sideways movement steers left/right. The shot is not automatically corrected toward the basket.

Finger tracking. Optional Hand Landmarker adds 21 finger joints per hand. Elbow extension triggers the shot; detected finger extension during release adjusts force and lateral aim. Without a visible hand, the game uses pose-only mechanics. This is a gesture-based approximation, not a measurement of true ball force.

Prototype note. Ball flight is a stylized simulation. It does not detect a real ball, spin, or true 3D release speed. Use good lighting and a side-ish angle that keeps your arm visible.

Privacy. Pose inference runs locally in your browser and no camera frames are uploaded. MediaPipe code and the model download from public hosting on first load. Camera access needs HTTPS or localhost.
