How to play: Face the camera, with your shooting arm in view. Bend your elbow to load your shot, then extend upward with a quick wrist motion. The game estimates release direction and power and sends a virtual ball toward the hoop. Get a few shots in, then adjust the sensitivity if release detection feels too hard or too easy.
Prototype note: The ball's flight is a stylized simulation based on the estimated motion. It does not detect an actual ball, wrist spin, or true 3D release velocity. For best tracking, use good lighting and a side-ish angle that keeps shoulder, elbow and wrist visible. Camera access requires localhost or HTTPS; MediaPipe model files need internet on the first load.
