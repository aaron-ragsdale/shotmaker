How to play: Bend your elbow to load the shot, then extend your arm upward with a quick wrist motion. The game estimates release angle and power, then launches a virtual ball toward the hoop. For the cleanest tracking, use good lighting and keep your shoulder, elbow, and wrist visible.

Privacy: pose inference runs locally in your browser. ShotMaker POV Arcade does not upload camera frames. MediaPipe JavaScript, WASM, and model files are downloaded from public hosting on first load.
